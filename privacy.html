Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

EZ Cam is a desktop application for Windows that records and edits videos on your own computer. This policy explains what information EZ Cam uses, when it goes online, and what happens when you connect it to YouTube.

Information on your computer

Your recordings, projects, settings and exported videos are stored only on your computer, in folders you can see and choose. EZ Cam does not send them anywhere unless you upload a video to YouTube yourself.

Speech recognition (transcripts), word timing, voice clean-up and webcam background effects all run on your computer. Your recordings are never sent anywhere to be processed.

If you turn on keystroke recording, the keys you press while recording are saved with that recording on your computer, so they can be shown as keystroke callouts. Ordinary typing is only kept if you also choose to include it.

When EZ Cam goes online

EZ Cam goes online only for the following. None of these send your recordings.

  • Updates: EZ Cam checks GitHub for a new version, and downloads it from GitHub when you choose to install it.
  • One-time downloads: the first time you use a feature that needs it, EZ Cam downloads the software or model it runs on your computer, and then works offline:
    • the speech recognition engine and model (from GitHub and Hugging Face), when you first transcribe;
    • the word-timing model (from Hugging Face), for precise word timing in transcripts;
    • the AI voice clean-up tool (DeepFilterNet, from GitHub), when you first use it;
    • the webcam background model (from Google), when you first blur or replace your camera background.
    These are ordinary file downloads: the services see a download request from your internet address, as with any download, and nothing else about you.
  • Text to speech with ElevenLabs (optional, with your own ElevenLabs account and API key): the text you want spoken and your chosen voice settings are sent to ElevenLabs, which returns the audio. ElevenLabs' own privacy policy applies to that text. Windows voices work offline and send nothing.
  • Ask Claude (optional, with your own Anthropic API key): the description you type, and when you ask for a change, the graphic being changed (its SVG code) and your requested change, are sent to Anthropic, whose Claude model returns the graphic. Nothing else is sent: no recordings, images or project files. Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to that text. Using it is billed to your Anthropic account.
  • YouTube (optional): described below.

Your ElevenLabs and Anthropic API keys are stored on your computer only, encrypted with Windows' own data protection, and are sent only to the service they belong to. You can replace a key at any time where you entered it.

YouTube and Google account data

Connecting EZ Cam to YouTube is optional. When you connect, you sign in with Google in your web browser and allow EZ Cam to:

  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload): only the videos you choose to upload, with the title, description, tags and settings you enter.
  • See your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): used only to show the name of the channel you are connected to.

EZ Cam does not read, change or delete your other videos, comments, subscriptions or any other account data.

How the data is stored and shared

  • The sign-in from Google is stored on your computer only, encrypted with Windows' own data protection. It is never sent to the developer of EZ Cam or to anyone other than Google.
  • Videos and their details go directly from your computer to YouTube. They do not pass through any other server.
  • EZ Cam has no accounts, analytics, tracking or advertising, and the developer receives no data about you or your use of the app. This applies to all of EZ Cam, not just YouTube.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose other than the upload you asked for.

EZ Cam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploading to YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Click Disconnect in EZ Cam (File → Settings → YouTube) to delete the stored sign-in and revoke EZ Cam's access. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Uninstalling EZ Cam and deleting its settings folder removes everything it stored, including the stored API keys and downloaded models.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.
